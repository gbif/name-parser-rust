## Genera and higher taxa
> A single word is a `uninomial`; its rank stays `UNRANKED` unless a suffix makes it certain (`-aceae` is always a family) or a rank is given as a hint.
Abies
Abies Mill.
Asteraceae
Carabidae Latreille, 1802
Lepidoptera Linnaeus, 1758
Pinus	L.

## Species
> A genus with a lower-case epithet is a species. The authorship is split into the combination authorship and, in brackets, the basionym authorship (the author of the original combination). A year with a comma and a bracketed original author are typical of zoology, a recombining author after the bracket of botany; both become evidence for the nomenclatural `code`.
Abies alba Mill.
Quercus robur L.
Homo sapiens Linnaeus, 1758
Puma concolor (Linnaeus, 1771)
Bellis perennis L.
Picea abies (L.) H.Karst.
Felis silvestris Schreber, 1777
Taraxacum officinale F.H.Wigg.
Abies alba	Mill.
Puma concolor	(Linnaeus, 1771)

## Infraspecific names
> A rank marker (`subsp.`, `var.`, `f.`, …) gives the rank of the last epithet. Without a marker, zoological trinomials are subspecies; otherwise the rank stays the generic `INFRASPECIFIC_NAME`. The author of the species, standing before the marker, is kept apart as the `specificAuthorship`. An autonym repeats the species epithet and has no author of its own.
Vulpes vulpes silaceus Miller, 1907
Poa pratensis subsp. angustifolia (L.) Lej.
Abies alba var. nana Carrière
Rosa canina f. dumetorum (Thuill.) Rouy
Festuca ovina L. subsp. guestfalica (Boenn. ex Rchb.) K.Richt.
Abies alba Mill. var. alba
Brassica oleracea var. capitata L.
Loranthus incanus Schumach. & Thonn. sessilis Sprague  # no rank marker
Hydnellum scrobiculatum var. zonatum f. parvum (Banker) D. Hall & D.E. Stuntz 1972  # the middle rank is dropped, with a warning
Agaricus collinitus β mucosus (Bull.) Fr.  # a Greek letter as an informal marker

## Subgenera and other infrageneric names
> A capitalised word in brackets after the genus is a subgenus. Infrageneric rank markers (`sect.`, `subg.`, `ser.`) work like the infraspecific ones; an author before the marker is the genus author.
Carabus (Carabus) auronitens Fabricius, 1792
Phalium (Semicassis) vector R. T. Abbott, 1993
Astragalus sect. Phaca
Centaurea L. subg. Jacea
Cordia (Adans.) Kuntze sect. Salimori
Hieracium unr. Verbasciformia  # unranked

## Hybrids
> The hybrid sign `×` marks the part of the name that is a nothotaxon (`notho`). A formula naming two parents is no name of its own.
Mentha × piperita L.
×Triticosecale Wittm.
Salix × rubens Schrank
Aconitum ×teppneri Mucher ex Starm. nothosubsp. goetzii Starm.
Salix alba × Salix fragilis

## Authors
> Authors are kept as written, with spacing around initials and dots normalised. Ex-authors proposed a name someone else published, an `in` citation goes to `publishedIn`. Particles, filius and all-capital names get their usual form.

## Sanctioned fungal names
> In mycology, Fries and Persoon "sanctioned" names, cited after a colon; inside a basionym's brackets for a sanctioned basionym.

Merulius lacrimans (Wulfen : Fr.) Schum.
Russula sanguinea (Bull. : Pers.) Fr. : Fr.

## Years
> The year is kept as written in the citation: a letter telling apart works of the same year, a question mark. A year in square brackets on its own was established from external evidence (`bracketedYear`); next to a plain year it is the year printed on the work, the imprint year.
Xyleborus muasi Browne, 1961a
Acalles giraudi Mulsant, 1875?
Phyllomacromia	Westwood, [1851]
Acleris aspersana (Hübner, [1814-1817])
Ctenotus alacer Storr, 1970 [1969]
Anomalopus truncatus (Peters, 1876 [1877])
Acleris logiana (Clerck, 1759 "1758")

## Anonymous authors
> An anonymously published work is `anonymous`; authors attributed to it from other evidence are cited in square brackets.
Rhinobatos typus (Anonymous [Bennett], 1830)
Apatura ilia ([Denis & Schiffermüller], 1775)
Physalospora rubiginosa (Fr.) anon.
Lupinus [Tourn.] L.  # a pre-Linnaean author, as ex-author

## Manuscript names and other ex citations
> A name taken from someone's manuscript (`MS`) and published by another author is an ex citation. A name that was never published (`ined.`, `ms.`) is a `manuscript` name.
Harpalus notatus Stephens (ex Kirby MS) 1828
Haustellum kiiensis Kuroda MS in Kira, 1959
Bacillus amylolyticus (ex Choukévitch 1911) Nakamura 1984  # a revived bacterial name
Eucnidoideae ined.
Abies alba Mill. ined.
Acosmeta	Moeschler, MS.
Oxalis barrelieri ined.?

> Notes on nomenclatural status are moved out of the authorship into `nomenclaturalNote`. A status that exists in one code only also sets the code.

## Taxonomic notes and references
> Concepts (`sensu`, `auct.`, `non`, `s.l.`) go to `taxonomicNote`; a citation of the publication to `publishedIn`, with its year in `publishedInYear`.

## Qualifiers and doubtful names
> Open nomenclature (`cf.`, `aff.`, `?`) qualifies an epithet; such a name is `INFORMAL` and the qualifier sits in `epithetQualifier`.

Pomatoschistus aff. pictus (Malm, 1865)
Ferganoconcha? oblonga
Buteo borealis ? ventralis
Sorex cf. S. shinto
